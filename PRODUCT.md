# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Visitors discovering Raphaël Raclot's projects or social profiles.

## Product Purpose
A personal entry point to raphaelraclot.dev, GitHub, LinkedIn and X. The existing portfolio remains the primary destination. The interactive passport is the experience, not a second portfolio.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js, React, Three.js, React Three Fiber and GSAP. The passport opens, turns pages and reveals UV markings. HTML destination links must remain immediately available, including while loading and without WebGL. Analytics use Upstash and must never block navigation. No production deployment is authorized by this redesign request.

## Brand Commitments
Raxuis and Raphaël Raclot. Preserve the original R logo and the French-passport-inspired burgundy cover and gold personal crest. The user chose: « Le passeport au centre, presque seul, avec quelques commandes discrètes ». Remove the editorial page framing, decorative prose, France Internet, Fait avec curiosité, pointer hints and Développeur web. No em dashes or slash separators in displayed copy.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's passport reference, original portrait public/avatar.jpg, R mark public/identity/raxuis-logo.png and the existing interactive scene. Destinations are verified from the user's statements and original site. No invented biography, metrics, endorsements or contact address.

## Product Principles
The object leads. Text names actions. Essential destinations remain usable without the scene. Brand decoration belongs to the physical passport rather than the surrounding interface.
