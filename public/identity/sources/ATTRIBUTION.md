# French passport emblem

Source: [French Passport Cover.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:French_Passport_Cover.svg), by **RainbowSilver2ndBackup and Futurhit12**, with subsequent file revisions by Wikimedia Commons contributors. Retrieved 17 November 2026 from https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/72/French_Passport_Cover.svg.

License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Adaptation for this project: extracted the emblem group, converted the original gold and burgundy shapes into a monochrome luminance mask, cropped the vector viewBox, reinforced the engraving by 0.35 source units for small display sizes and applied a metallic material in the scene. The original personal R crest is replaced on the cover. The header and favicon retain the owner's logo. No endorsement is implied.
