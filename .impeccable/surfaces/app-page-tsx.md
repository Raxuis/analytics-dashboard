# Passport surface

Scope: root homepage; visitor mode Experience. This is the user-authorized replacement of the editorial wrapper. The passport, actual links and working behavior are preserved.

THESIS: A single French-inspired personal passport sits in the middle of a neutral stage. Reject the split hero, giant name, small decorative labels, editorial captions and row of marketing cards.

OWN-WORLD: Burgundy leather and gold remain inside the object. The surrounding interface is neutral charcoal #141416, soft white #f3f3f4, secondary #b5b5bd, control surface #242428, hover #323237. DM Sans is the only surrounding typeface, 13–16px. No large display type, decorative monospace, slogans, gradients or header rules.

STORY: See the passport, click the cover to open it, drag to turn pages, inspect country maps and city tooltips, choose a link. Visitors can also open a compact nonmodal link list directly from the header.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full viewport. Original R mark and a small Raxuis label at top left. One compact Liens button at top right. The actual 3D passport occupies the center of the available stage. A small functional toolbar below contains UV and opening or page controls. No main column, explanatory footer, dividing rules, numbered editorial sections or cards. Loading uses one label and real resource progress in the object region. A simple view preserves direct links.

FORM: seed: none (pinned). User direction: « Le passeport au centre, presque seul, avec quelques commandes discrètes ». The user explicitly delegated choice and execution; build directly in code using the approved existing 3D object.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Découverte initiale

Une seule démonstration brève par geste : couverture légèrement soulevée, coin de page courbé, onde sur une ville réelle. Les indications textuelles sont contextualisées et mémorisées séparément. Le visiteur garde la main, les mouvements ne tournent aucune page et sont interrompus au toucher ou au clic. Les overlays et transitions suspendent les indices ; Reduce Motion conserve uniquement le texte.
